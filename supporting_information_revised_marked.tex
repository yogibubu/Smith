\section{Standalone Input and Output Reference}

\subsection{Input schema \texttt{matrix.smith.input.v1}}

The standalone boundary is a standard Cartesian XYZ block followed by optional
\SMITH\ directives.  Four provider-neutral profiles are accepted.  Their
meaning is fixed by the input contract rather than by the program that created
the data.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\small
\caption{The four standalone input profiles.}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.24\linewidth}X@{}}
\toprule
Profile & Required content and action \\
\midrule
\texttt{FROZEN\_STATE} & A complete validated molecular state.  Topology,
symmetry operations, atom permutations, primitive rows and provenance are
consumed unchanged. \\
\texttt{STANDALONE\_TOPOLOGY} & Cartesian geometry and a supplied
\texttt{TOPOLOGY} section.  The pinned standalone frontend constructs the
ordinary redundant primitives. \\
\texttt{STANDALONE\_PRIMITIVES} & Cartesian geometry and a supplied
\texttt{PRIMITIVES} section.  Values, atom indices, Wilson rows and geometry
fingerprints are validated before SONIC construction. \\
\texttt{STANDALONE\_MINIMAL} & Plain Cartesian geometry.  The pinned frontend
constructs covalent topology, cycles, point-group operations with atom
permutations and ordinary primitives. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

If \texttt{TOPOLOGY} and \texttt{PRIMITIVES} are both present, their bond rows
must agree.  The output records the selected profile, the source fingerprints
and the accepted construction options.  The minimal route is sufficient for
ordinary molecules; interaction centers, externally audited quasi-symmetry or
special primitives can be supplied through the richer profiles.  This keeps
\SMITH\ operationally standalone while making the perception/construction
boundary explicit.

\subsection{Formic-acid--water fragment contract}

The eight-atom complex is processed with an automatic connected-component
fragment definition and closes at rank \(18/18\).  Six protected rows describe
the three relative translations and three relative orientations; the remaining
twelve rows span the intramolecular motions.  Every build writes the frozen
\texttt{xyzin}, a readable coordinate report and the optional Gaussian input.

\textcolor{red}{The symmetry-projected fragment path is also checked with
exact-family-quota regressions in \(C_s\), \(C_{2v}\), \(C_{3v}\), and
\(C_i\).  Selection is the intersection of the linear matroid defined by
independent analytic Wilson rows and the partition matroid defined by the
native SONIC family dimensions.  The tests require full rank, unchanged counts
for every family, three relative translations and three relative orientations
per additional nonlinear fragment, assigned irreps, and successful Cartesian
covariance under every stored operation.  The \(C_{2v}\), \(C_{3v}\), and
\(C_i\) probes are algorithmic symmetry fixtures rather than optimized

\section{Domain-First Special-Coordinate Construction}

\subsection{Ownership order and conservative recognition}

The construction order tested in this work is
\[
\text{stretches}\longrightarrow\text{special-domain inventory}
\longrightarrow\text{complete special blocks}
\longrightarrow\text{ordinary residual chart}.
\]
The special inventory contains ring and butterfly domains before local
\(XY_n\) domains, so an atom or primitive already owned by a cyclic source
space cannot be reclassified by a center rule.  \(XY_3\) requires a
four-coordinate acyclic center with three equivalent \(Y\) branches and one
distinct \(Z\) branch.  \(XY_2\) requires a three-coordinate center satisfying
the analogous (2+1) partition, and the center must not belong to a cycle.
Terminal atoms of the same element are exactly permutable.  Nonterminal
branches must additionally have identical rooted graph environments after
removing the center.  A fully symmetric \(XY_4\) or \(XY_3\) center has no
unique \(Z\) and is therefore not relabelled as \(XY_3-Z\) or \(XY_2-Z\),
respectively.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{\(XY_3\) and \(XY_2\)}{XY3 and XY2} coordinate blocks}

For \(X(Y_1Y_2Y_3)-Z\), the six primitive valence angles comprise three
\(ZXY_a\) and three \(Y_aXY_b\) rows.  \SMITH\ retains a five-dimensional
orthonormal local bending block: two \(E\)-type combinations of the
\(ZXY_a\) orbit, the symmetric \(A_1\)-type combination of the
\(Y_aXY_b\) orbit, and two \(E\)-type combinations of that orbit.  The
missing symmetric angular direction is redundant with the bonded local frame.
The torsional coordinate is the common rotation
\[
q_{\mathrm{rot}}^{XY_3}=(3N_W)^{-1/2}
\sum_{w\in W(Z)}\sum_{a=1}^{3}D(w,Z,X,Y_a).
\]
Thus the two differential directions present in a three-branch torsional span
are carried by the local deformation block rather than duplicated as rotor
coordinates.

For an acyclic \(X(Y_1Y_2)-Z\) center, the owned angular block contains
\[
\frac{2A(Y_1,X,Y_2)-A(Z,X,Y_1)-A(Z,X,Y_2)}{\sqrt{6}},
\qquad
\frac{A(Z,X,Y_1)-A(Z,X,Y_2)}{\sqrt{2}},
\]
together with the oriented out-of-plane row of the same center.  Its collective
rotor is
\[
q_{\mathrm{rot}}^{XY_2}=(2N_W)^{-1/2}
\sum_{w\in W(Z)}\sum_{a=1}^{2}D(w,Z,X,Y_a).
\]
The expected periodicities are three and two for equivalent terminal
\(XY_3\) and \(XY_2\) groups.  Heterosubstitution, a cyclic \(XY_2\)
center, a linear branch or the absence of an admissible \(W\) anchor triggers
a recorded \texttt{ONEDIH} fallback.

\subsection{Executable interface and regression gates}

The optional collective-domain path is requested explicitly:
\begin{lstlisting}
python -m matrix smith gicforge build molecule.xyzin \
  --xy3-torsions --xy2-torsions
\end{lstlisting}
The independent Fortran control uses the corresponding \texttt{XY3} and
\texttt{XY2} keywords.  Both implementations store normalized coefficients,
domain atoms, anchors, term count, periodicity and fallback reason.  Regression
tests require identical ordered primitive charts and Wilson rows for the
Python/Fortran \(XY_3\) control, a six-term normalized \(XY_2\) rotor for the
methylamine control, exclusive ownership of the \(XY_2\) out-of-plane row,
and rejection of cyclic, heterosubstituted and non-unique-\(Z\) motifs.  The
established fused, bridged, spiro and cage corpus is rerun to ensure that the
new center domains do not alter ring ownership or final vibrational rank.
}

\section{Gaussian 16 Serialization Details}

\subsection{\texttt{ReadAllGIC} route and generated syntax}

Gaussian 16 is used as an external interpreter because its GIC syntax accepts
general symbolic coordinate expressions.  The SONIC blocks examined here are
one particular application of that substantially broader syntax; they do not
define or delimit the other coordinate models and workflows supported by GIC.
All calculations and parser tests in this Supporting Information use the
commercial Gaussian 16 Rev. C.01 installation.  The broader GIC functionality
reported by Marenich et al. was implemented and tested in a development version
of the Gaussian suite; those new capabilities are scientifically interesting
but were not available to, and were not used in, the present validation.
The compatibility route has the form

\begin{lstlisting}
#p method/basis opt=(readallgic,calcfc,maxcycle=80)
\end{lstlisting}

\texttt{CalcFC} is optional and was used for the direct coordinate-choice
comparison, whereas the three-system solver-consumption table in the main text
uses no initial force-constant calculation.  The generated block is dependency
closed: helper centers and fragment axes precede every expression that uses
them, inactive helpers are emitted as frozen functions, and the complete
rank-closing coordinate set is retained.

\subsection{Terminal translation of native out-of-plane rows}

The native SONIC contract uses the signed curvilinear out-of-plane coordinate

\[
U(j;a,b,c)=\arcsin\!\left[
\frac{\hat r_{ja}\cdot(\hat r_{jb}\times\hat r_{jc})}
{|\hat r_{jb}\times\hat r_{jc}|}\right],
\]

with the stored atom order fixing the sign.  Gaussian 16 Rev. C.01 has no
native \(U\) primitive, so serialization alone applies
\(U(I,J,K,L)\mapsto D(J,I,L,K)\).  Multi-\(U\) ring-puckering combinations
remain in the frozen SONIC contract, while the Gaussian file receives their
component periodic dihedrals.  The translation does not redefine the native
coordinate model.  When molecular symmetry is retained, all non-totally
symmetric SONIC rows are written with \texttt{Frozen}; for norbornane this
leaves 15 active \(A_1\) rows and 36 frozen rows in the full 51-coordinate
contract.

The generated butterfly is
\begin{equation}
 AppBtFl001=2^{-1/2}\!\left[D(6,1,2,13)-D(16,1,2,3)\right],
\end{equation}
whose relative minus sign supplies the inter-ring direction missing from the
five \(RPck\) rows.  Replacing it by the corresponding sum lowers the rank of
these six rows from six to five.  The regression therefore checks the complete
post-symmetry Cartesian \(\Bmat\), rather than accepting six individually
well-formed but collectively dependent expressions.

Central finite differences of every final SONIC row give maximum absolute
analytic-row errors of \(4.02\times10^{-10}\) for pagodane and
\(4.77\times10^{-10}\) for indane, and \(3.91\times10^{-10}\) for saccharin.
The indane and saccharin sets contain stretch, bend,
cyclic-bend, butterfly and ring-puckering-component families.  All 114 pagodane
and 51 indane display motions are constructed in both signs with the Wilson
matrix frozen at the reference geometry.  They are first-order visualization
artifacts rather than nonlinear internal-to-Cartesian realization services.

Extended independent use by collaborators exposed an important earlier
failure mode.  A dihedral-based representation of endocyclic out-of-plane
deformation did not converge in the back-transformation of a near-planar fused
indane/indene-type scaffold containing one \(sp^3\) CH\(_2\) center.  The
ill-conditioning belonged to the collectively redundant cyclic-dihedral chart
at planarity, not to SONIC rank or symmetry construction.  A native signed
\(U\) source has a finite first-order row at the planar reference and avoids
the dihedral branch ambiguity.  Symmetry projection acts on this oriented
row space and retains both source and target phases; the complete \(\Bmat\)
rank is then checked across all coordinate families.  The indane and
saccharin fixtures are therefore complementary regressions for the change
prompted by independent use.

The version-2 diagnostic manifest records the right-inverse metric, mass
provenance, coordinate conditioning, local domain and pseudogroup, independent
local/global total-symmetry labels, canonical ring order and primitive
coefficients.  A distributed JSON Schema and dependency-free validator define
the machine contract, and the reader remains compatible with archived
version-1 manifests.
