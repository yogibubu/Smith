\opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief,}

Please find enclosed the manuscript ``SONIC: Symmetry-Oriented Non-redundant
Internal Coordinates,'' submitted for consideration as an Article in the
\emph{Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation}.

The manuscript addresses a persistent constructive problem in computational
chemistry: how to obtain a chemically interpretable, non-redundant and
symmetry-adapted internal-coordinate model without molecule-specific coordinate
editing or arbitrary cross-family weights. It introduces SONIC and its SMITH
implementation, which organize ordinary and special internal coordinates in
homogeneous families, protect ring and fragment semantics, enforce analytic
Wilson-B rank closure, and apply local and exact molecular symmetry before
freezing a portable coordinate contract.

The work is aligned with the scope of JCTC because it presents new computational
methodology together with an independently installable implementation and
quantitative validation. The tests span acyclic, cyclic, fused, bridged,
high-symmetry, high-coordination, non-covalent and metal--ligand systems. They
include analytic-versus-finite-difference derivative checks, rigid-motion and
atom-permutation covariance, local-SALC validation, serialization of one SONIC
coordinate model through the general Gaussian 16 GIC syntax, and ORCA/Molpro
cross-code probes. The frozen SONIC contract is a
reusable representation for downstream optimization, vibrational, force-field
and structural-refinement methods, whose algorithms remain explicitly outside
the scope of the present construction paper.

An earlier version of the manuscript has been submitted to arXiv and is
currently awaiting processing and assignment of its public identifier. The
present journal version records substantial post-upload improvements: it
uses signed curvilinear native out-of-plane sources for ring construction,
corrects phase-covariant symmetry projection and full-rank treatment of
puckering coordinates, adds the independent indane and saccharin regression
cases, expands the provider-neutral standalone interfaces, and freezes the
validated SMITH 0.1.0rc7 implementation and reproducibility bundle. The arXiv
record will be linked to the journal article if the manuscript is published.

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not
under consideration elsewhere. I am the sole author. No external funding was
received for this work, and I declare no competing financial interest. The
source, versioned standalone package, validation data and reproduction scripts
are available through the repository identified in the manuscript.

Thank you for considering this submission.
